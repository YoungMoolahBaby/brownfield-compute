# 22 05 29: Hangers and supports for the water pipes, held off the frame

Made up, from the brownfield-compute study: not a real project, nothing is out to bid. Issued by Addendum 9.

## 1. What it covers

The PEX water pipe above the slab, cold and hot and the hot water's return, risers included: 2,417 ft in the services model. The mains under the slab come pre-insulated (P-601).

## 2. Supports

- Support the pipe as IPC 2021 Table 308.5 asks: PEX 1 in. and smaller every 2.67 ft (32 in.) horizontally, 1-1/4 in. and larger every 4 ft; vertically at each floor, with a guide midway between (its note b).
- Where the pipe passes through studs, plates or joists, the hole is a support; between holes farther apart than the table's spacing, hang or strap it.

## 3. Held off the frame

No water pipe bears on wood or metal above the slab. The design's quiet supply pipes depend on it: Warnock and Morin (NRC, 1990) measured plastic pipe in closed-cell elastomeric sleeves 13 dB below the same pipe clamped rigidly.

- **At every hanger and strap:** the clamp goes over a closed-cell elastomeric sleeve, 1/2 in. wall, the pipe's size, at least 6 in. long. The clamp holds the sleeve, not the pipe. About 348 in the model's runs.
- **At every hole of the cold pipe:** the pipe passes in the same sleeve, slid along to the member. The routes bored these holes for the pipe in 1/2 in. of insulation, so they take it as bored: 308 holes.
- **At every hole of the hot pipe and its return:** an acoustic through-stud isolator, snapped into the hole (basis of design Holdrite SILENCER 261, 262 and 263). Bore the hole to the isolator: 1-3/8 in. for 1/2 and 3/4 in. pipe, 2 in. for 1 in. pipe. These holes were bored for the bare pipe, and a sleeve would overfill 56 of them beyond what their joists allow. The isolator fits all 452: the holes schedule's sizes for these runs are superseded, each new size within what the schedule allows the member.
- **The hot pipe's insulation** (IECC C404.4, 1 in.) runs to each side of the isolator, as it stops at each member now.
- **The count,** from the model: 656 sleeves and 452 isolators (170 for 1/2 in. pipe, 150 for 3/4 in. pipe, 132 for 1 in. pipe).

## 4. Submittals

- The sleeve's material and wall, and the isolator's data sheet, each before it's bought.

## 5. Inspection

- Before the walls are closed, the plumber shows each flat's water pipes, and the inspector or the owner's representative looks for any pipe bearing on the frame: at the holes, the hangers and the straps.
