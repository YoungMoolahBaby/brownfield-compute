## Sites and data

- **EIA-860M, Monthly Update to the Annual Electric Generator Report**, September 2026 data,
  released 2026-10-23 (next 2026-11-24), https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia860m/. Every
  US generator of 1 MW and up: operating (with planned retirement year), retired (with retirement
  year), planned, canceled. Early-release data, "not yet validated". Pass 1's plant universe.
- **TVA and Type One Energy, Bull Run** (2025-2026 reporting): TVA retired the coal-fired Bull
  Run Fossil Plant (Anderson County, TN, next to Oak Ridge) in December 2023 and signed a letter
  of intent in September 2025 to explore Type One's fusion technology there once commercially
  ready; Infinity Two is described as a 350 MWe stellarator plant (some outlets say 400), aimed
  at the mid-2030s; the site is planned as a fusion campus with ORNL and the University of
  Tennessee. Sources: [Power Engineering](https://www.power-eng.com/nuclear/tva-signs-loi-to-explore-deploying-350-mw-fusion-plant-at-former-coal-site/),
  [Type One Energy](https://typeoneenergy.com/insights-announcements/tva-type-one-energy-accelerate-fusion-commercialization-tennessee/),
  [World Nuclear News](https://world-nuclear-news.org/articles/tva-type-one-energy-collaborate-on-fusion-commercialisation).
  Use: a known case for the site screen; pass 5's plant size.
- **Homer City, PA** (announced April 2025): the former coal plant, Pennsylvania's largest, to
  become a gas-powered data center campus, "up to" 4.5 GW from seven GE Vernova turbines, ~$10
  billion for power and site, ~3,200 acres, transmission already in place, power from ~2027;
  developers project 10,000+ construction jobs and ~1,000 permanent (power plant and campus
  together; developer figures, no tenant named). Sources:
  [AP via WSLS](https://www.wsls.com/tech/2025/04/02/pennsylvanias-largest-coal-fired-power-plant-now-retired-to-become-gas-powered-data-center-campus/),
  [EnergyTech](https://www.energytech.com/distributed-energy/article/55279223/kiewit-ge-vernova-converting-pennsylvania-coal-plant-to-4-gw-gas-fired-electricity-for-data-centers).
  A known case for the site screen. Its jobs figure is far above the "few dozen to a few
  hundred" of a campus alone (D4): to check against built campuses in pass 4.
- **Homer City, the AWS data center campus** (Project Narrative, Homer City Energy Campus, filed
  with the Indiana County Office of Planning and Development, PDF dated 2026-08-27,
  [icopd.org](https://icopd.org/assets/pit100_project-narrative_082726.pdf)): Amazon Data
  Services proposes to acquire ~2,200 acres in Center and Blacklick Townships and build 39 data
  centers on six sites (Bruner, Helen, Green, West, Harvey/Gaston, Rura); two storeys (~70 ft),
  ~112,500 sq ft footprint, 225,000 sq ft per building; "1,500 to 2,000 permanent operations
  staff" at full build-out, average salaries $80,000-$100,000; 50 employees per building at the
  peak shift (parking); phased from December 2026 to Q2-Q3 2036 (Helen last); power bought from
  FirstEnergy, "supported by the 4.5-gigawatt natural gas power generation facility being
  constructed by HCR"; "Non-contact cooling water will be supplied through existing
  water-withdrawal rights ... returned to the Homer City Station for reuse ... the combined
  campus operations are projected to maintain the historic water withdrawal rate"; a Community
  Benefits Agreement to be developed "consistent with Governor Shapiro's GRID standards"; AWS
  "does not anticipate seeking any economic incentives that would reduce real property tax
  assessments". The data center cooling design and IT megawatts per building are not stated.
- **Homer City water** (Homer City Generation fact sheet, October 2026,
  [homercityredevelopment.com](https://www.homercityredevelopment.com/post/homer-city-generation-publishes-fact-sheet-on-campus-water-usage)):
  source stays the Two Lick Reservoir via Two Lick Creek, owned and operated by the company;
  campus-wide use (power plant and data centers) "in line with the historical usage levels of
  the former coal plant"; the new plant's maximum intake rate "5.9 gpm/MW" vs "14 gpm/MW" for
  the coal plant; on-site treatment, "hundreds of millions of dollars". Cooling methods not
  specified. Derived, not stated: if per MW of the 4.5 GW plant, 5.9 gpm/MW is ~26,600 gpm,
  ~38 million gallons a day at the maximum intake rate.
- **Homer City water permit** (CNHI/Indiana Gazette via
  [Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/homer-city-generations-data-center-035900411.html)):
  an NPDES amendment would allow up to 6.8 million gallons a day (7 million per one speaker)
  from one pipe into the confluence of Two Lick and Blacklick Creeks; a hearing slide showed
  discharge up to 110 F; DEP public hearing 2026-10-14 at Homer-Center High School, no decision
  yet. The coal plant drew 5.6 million gallons a day from Two Lick Creek in 2020 (DEP data).
  Pennsylvania American Water draws ~2.2 million gallons a day upstream for Indiana Borough and
  White Township. Blacklick Creek Watershed Association (30+ years of cleanup) and Trout
  Unlimited (thermal impact) raised concerns; DEP opened a $27 million+ mine drainage treatment
  plant at Vintondale in 2025, improving ~25 stream miles of Blacklick Creek.
- **Naughton, WY** is not a known case: EIA-860M (September 2026) lists units 1-2 as coal with
  no planned retirement and unit 3 converted to gas; TerraPower's Natrium plant is being built
  next to a plant that still runs.
- **Interconnection rights after retirement (PJM)**: a deactivated unit's capacity
  interconnection rights terminate one year after its deactivation date unless the holder has
  submitted a completed interconnection request within that year; the replacement process (FERC
  accepted PJM's revised tariff effective 2026-01-30, after rejecting an earlier version in
  August 2025) needs a request within one year of deactivation and an in-service date generally
  within three years; rights are capped at the original facility's and need not stay at the
  same point of interconnection. MISO and SPP have had expedited generator replacement since
  2019 (FERC order cited in PJM's filing; their deadlines `?`). Sources:
  [PJM Inside Lines](https://insidelines.pjm.com/ferc-approves-streamlined-process-to-replace-retiring-facilities/),
  [FERC order ER26-403-001](https://www.pjm.com/pjmfiles/directory/etariff/FercOrders/8585/20260129-er26-403-001.pdf).
  For pass 1: generating on site (gas, fusion) needs a site still retiring or retired within a
  year; a data center that only draws power connects as load, where the lines and substation
  the plant leaves matter, not these rights.

## Building codes and lumber (for pass 3)

- **Minn. R. 1303.1600** (footing depth for frost protection): 5 ft in Zone I (34 northern
  counties incl. St. Louis, Carlton, Cook, Lake), 3.5 ft in Zone II (53 counties incl. Hennepin,
  Ramsey, Dakota). https://www.revisor.mn.gov/rules/1303.1600/
- **Minn. R. 1303.1700** (ground snow load): 60 psf in a list of northern counties (incl. St.
  Louis, Pine, Polk, Roseau), 50 psf in all others (2014 text, listed current through 2024).
  https://regulations.justia.com/states/minnesota/agency-151/chapter-1303/part-1303-1700
- **SFPA, Maximum Span Comparisons by Species** (2013), spans at 16 in. o.c. from 2012 NDS
  Supplement values for SPF and Hem-Fir:
  [PDF](https://www.southernpine.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/B_Maximum-Span-Comparisons-by-Species_060113.pdf).
  Floor joists, 10 psf dead, L/360: 30 psf live 2x10 No.1/No.2 SPF 17-2; 40 psf live 2x12
  No.1/No.2 SPF 17-10. Ceiling joists L/240: 10/5 psf 2x6 No.2 SPF 16-11; 20/10 psf 2x8 No.2 SPF
  16-3. Rafters 20/10 psf L/240: C_D 1.15 2x8 No.2 SPF 17-5; C_D 1.25 2x6 No.2 SPF 13-5. Both
  floor cases reproduced by hand with SPF No.1/No.2 Fb 875 psi, E 1.4e6 psi, C_F 1.1 (2x10) /
  1.0 (2x12), C_r 1.15: 2x10 at 30 psf 206.9 in. by deflection, 206.4 in. by bending (17-2);
  2x12 at 40 psf 214.1 in. by bending (17-10). The NDS values are from memory, to confirm
  against the AWC Supplement Table 4A before pass 3 relies on them.

## Pass 2 inputs (firmed up 2026-11-15)

- **Water price**: York Water (PA) industrial tariff effective 2025-08-01, $7.755 per 1,000 gal in
  the first tier and $4.338 above 2,000,000 gal a month
  ([tariff](https://www.yorkwater.com/wp-content/uploads/165-Pro-Forma-Rate-Case-Water-Tariff-Effective-August-1-2025.pdf)).
  Pennsylvania American Water's industrial rates sit in its PUC tariff (Rate Zone 1, docket
  R-2025-3057983), not read.
- **Cooling tower fan efficiency**: ASHRAE 90.1 Table 6.8.1-7, open-circuit axial-fan towers
  >= 40.2 gpm/hp at 95 F in, 85 F out, 75 F wet bulb (BAC codes and standards summary,
  [PDF](https://baltimoreaircoil.com/es/download_api_endpoint/1187/j22_tr_codesstandards_20150914.pdf);
  older editions 38.2). Fans <= 0.0127 kW per kW of heat at rating (derived).
- **AI rack coolant**: GB200 NVL72 maximum liquid inlet 45 C, maximum return 65 C, up to 130 L/min
  per rack ([QCT](https://blog.qct.io/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/QCT-Qoolrack-Stand-Alone_Advanced-Liquid-Cooling-for-NVIDIA-GB200-NVL72-Systems.pdf));
  NVIDIA: 45 C lets the facility reject heat without chillers in many climates
  ([NVIDIA blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/liquid-cooling-ai-factories/)); Uptime Institute:
  most customers keep chillers and run colder.
- **AWS fleet**: PUE 1.15 and WUE 0.15 L/kWh (2024), 1.14 and 0.12 (2025)
  ([2024 AWS summary](https://sustainability.aboutamazon.com/2024-amazon-sustainability-report-aws-summary.pdf),
  [Georgia fact sheet](https://sustainability.aboutamazon.com/aws-sustainability-fact-sheets/aws-fact-sheet-georgia.pdf)).
- **Homes around the campus**: Indiana County parcels, November 2023, PASDA map service layer 5
  ([REST](https://mapservices.pasda.psu.edu/server/rest/services/pasda/IndianaCounty/MapServer/5)):
  owner field `LAST_NAME` (Homer City Generation LP), dwelling type `DWELLING_T`. OpenStreetMap
  had only 29 buildings within 3 km and wasn't usable.
- **Wet bulb**: Stull, R. (2011), "Wet-bulb temperature from relative humidity and air
  temperature", J. Appl. Meteor. Climatol. 50, 2267-2269: 20 C at 50% gives 13.7 C (a test).
