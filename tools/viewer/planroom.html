Why this exists. Bid sets go out as PDFs, and every bidder rebuilds the building from them before they can price it. This page puts the drawings and the model side by side, as a plan room could. What each costs a bidder is in the study's report, the bid set as data.

The model opens in your browser; nothing is uploaded. The files are generated from the study's open model and checked against it by two independent readers; the code is public.

The viewer uses That Open's web-ifc 0.0.78 (MPL-2.0: source, licence) and three.js r160 (MIT: source, licence), unchanged.
